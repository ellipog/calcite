## Who needs one

You need a host license if your business gives customers a Calcite server of their own, whether it's rented, free with ads or paid tiers, or run for them. That includes:

- offering Calcite preinstalled, as a one-click install, or as a choice of server software;
- promoting Calcite to your customers;
- renting out worlds that customers administer as servers of their own, with their own operators, plugins or settings.

You don't need one to:

- run your own servers or network, even with paid ranks or access, or with islands, plots and claims that players manage;
- host servers for friends or a community, if you charge nothing and gain nothing commercially from it;
- rent out machines or server slots that customers install Calcite on themselves;
- make control panels or other server software.

Calcite versions past their Change Date, two years after they were published, are MIT and need no license.
