# Villagers with their brains (CAL-160), on the flat world's grass in chunk (0, 0) in the
# morning, when their schedules have them idle: Ada, grown with no profession, five to nine blocks
# east of the bot's spawn, with a white bed by her, which she claims as her home, and Bo, a baby,
# eleven to fifteen blocks east and south. Each is ringed by oak fences, which `fill` sets as lone
# posts with gaps a villager slips through, so they wander out: Ada comes within the 4 a villager
# shows a player its trades from, the bot's hand empty, but never within the 8 of Bo she'd go to
# him from. No job site is near, so neither takes a profession. Summoned with their data, so
# nothing's drawn for their spawn, `finalizeSpawn`. Natural spawning's off in the session, so
# nothing else comes. As in `armadillo.txt`, the chunks within the simulation distance of 3 of the
# spawn but (0, 0) are forced, so both servers tick the same chunks from the bot's join and their
# draws line up, and the villagers' chunk ticks them from the join.

summon minecraft:villager 6.5 -60 1.5 {PersistenceRequired:1b,CustomName:"Ada",Rotation:[90.0f,0.0f]}
