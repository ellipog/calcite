# (0, 0), on normal, which keeps monsters, at midnight, so the sun doesn't burn him: Bones, named
# and persistent, ten blocks east of the spawn, facing west. Summoned with his data, so nothing's
# drawn for his spawn, `finalizeSpawn`, the bow his data gives him read as he's loaded, which

summon minecraft:skeleton 10.5 -60 0.5 {PersistenceRequired:1b,CustomName:"Bones",Rotation:[90.0f,0.0f],equipment:{mainhand:{id:"minecraft:bow",count:1}}}
