# Villagers without AI (CAL-160), on the flat world's grass in chunk (0, 0), by the bot's spawn:
# Ada, grown with no profession, two blocks east; Bo, a baby, two blocks south; and Fen, a desert
# farmer at level 2 with 60 of the 70 experience level 3 takes (CAL-163), two blocks south-east. All
# are in the one chunk, so both servers give them their IDs in the order the chunk saved them,
# whichever chunk loads first (CAL-199). They're summoned with their data and without AI, so their
# brains never tick; a summon with data doesn't finalize its spawn, so Ada's and Bo's data stays the
# default, a plains villager with no profession at level 1, and isn't final.

summon minecraft:villager 2.5 -60 0.5 {NoAI:1b,PersistenceRequired:1b,CustomName:"Ada",Rotation:[90.0f,0.0f]}
summon minecraft:villager 0.5 -60 2.5 {NoAI:1b,PersistenceRequired:1b,CustomName:"Bo",Age:-24000,Rotation:[180.0f,0.0f]}
summon minecraft:villager 2.5 -60 2.5 {NoAI:1b,PersistenceRequired:1b,CustomName:"Fen",VillagerData:{type:"minecraft:desert",profession:"minecraft:farmer",level:2},Xp:60,Rotation:[135.0f,0.0f]}
