# Arrows that hit blocks some 85 ticks after the world loads, after the levels are seeded again,
# each block doing something as it's hit, `onProjectileHit` (CAL-159), all in chunk (0, 0), on
# normal at midnight: one shot straight up falls back onto the middle of a target block's top,
# which gives its whole signal for 20 ticks; one onto a big dripleaf, which tilts all the way
# down, its tilt down's pitch drawn from the level's random, and lets the arrow fall through; and
# one with no gravity, shot north along the ground at a tenth of a block a tick, slowing as it
# goes, into the south side of a bell on the floor that faces north, which rings. The bot stands
# at the spawn, the middle of the chunk. The target stands on dirt, as grass under it would die
# on random ticks before the levels are seeded again, which aren't the same on both servers
# (CAL-164).
setworldspawn 8 -60 8
difficulty normal
time set 18000
setblock 12 -61 4 minecraft:dirt
setblock 12 -60 4 minecraft:target
summon minecraft:arrow 12.5 -56.5 4.5 {Motion:[0.0d,2.2d,0.0d]}
setblock 4 -60 4 minecraft:big_dripleaf[facing=north]
summon minecraft:arrow 4.5 -56.5 4.5 {Motion:[0.0d,2.2d,0.0d]}
setblock 10 -60 1 minecraft:bell[attachment=floor,facing=north]
summon minecraft:arrow 10.5 -59.6 7.5 {Motion:[0.0d,0.0d,-0.1d],NoGravity:1b}
