# A player dying in a wall and respawning (D427, D428): `buried`'s two blocks of stone where the
# bot stands at the spawn, and the four chunks around it forced, so what the bot drops stays
# loaded and ticking between its leaving its level and its respawn. Without them each server
# unloads those chunks as it likes once the bot's tickets go, vanilla as its saves finish, and
# reads the dropped items and orbs back with new IDs.
setworldspawn 0 -60 0
fill 0 -60 0 0 -59 0 minecraft:stone
forceload add -32 -32 31 31
