# Villagers at work (CAL-160), on the flat world's grass in the morning, when their schedules have
# them work: Gi, a librarian, by her lectern, with an offer used 5 times, which she restocks as
# she works; Ha, a cleric, by his brewing stand, with no offers yet and his last restock more than
# half a day back, which his work makes his offers for, a new day, their demand caught up; Fa, a
# farmer, by her composter, her inventory empty and one restock today, too soon for another; and
# Ib, a mason, thirteen blocks from his stonecutter, which he walks back to. Each one's saved
# memories hold its job site, and no other villager seeks one, so none looks for another. Summoned
# with their data, so nothing's drawn for their spawn. Natural spawning's off in the session. As in

# are forced, so both servers tick the same chunks from the bot's join and their draws line up;
# all four are in the one chunk, (0, 0), so both servers read them, and give them their IDs and
# places in the tick, in the order they were saved.
