# Villagers showing a player the trades their item pays for (CAL-160), on the flat world's grass in
# chunk (0, 0) in the morning, when their schedules have grown villagers with no job site idle: the
# bot, holding emeralds, in a pen walled two high in stone with Fen and Gil, farmers at the first
# level with some experience, so neither loses the profession for want of a job site. Fen's offers
# are saved: bread and apples for an emerald each, and an emerald for wheat; Gil has none made.
# Within 4 of the bot, each looks at it and shows it its trades, `ShowTradesToPlayer`: Fen holds up
# the bread and the apples in turn, 40 ticks each, as the emeralds pay for them; Gil's offers are
# made as it first looks for what they show, `getOffers`, drawn from the farmer's trade set, and it
# holds up what they give for emeralds, if any. Summoned with their data, so nothing's drawn for
# their spawn. Natural spawning's off in the session. As in `villagers.txt`, the chunks within the
# simulation distance of 3 of the spawn but (0, 0) are forced, so both servers tick the same chunks
# from the bot's join and their draws line up.
setworldspawn 0 -60 0
time set 1000
forceload add -48 -48 63 63
fill -3 -60 -3 5 -59 -3 minecraft:stone
fill -3 -60 5 5 -59 5 minecraft:stone
fill -3 -60 -2 -3 -59 4 minecraft:stone
fill 5 -60 -2 5 -59 4 minecraft:stone
summon minecraft:villager 3.5 -60 0.5 {PersistenceRequired:1b,CustomName:"Fen",VillagerData:{profession:"minecraft:farmer",level:1,type:"minecraft:plains"},Xp:1,Offers:{Recipes:[{buy:{id:"minecraft:emerald",count:1},sell:{id:"minecraft:bread",count:6},maxUses:16,xp:1,priceMultiplier:0.05f},{buy:{id:"minecraft:emerald",count:1},sell:{id:"minecraft:apple",count:4},maxUses:16,xp:1,priceMultiplier:0.05f},{buy:{id:"minecraft:wheat",count:20},sell:{id:"minecraft:emerald",count:1},maxUses:16,xp:2,priceMultiplier:0.05f}]},Rotation:[90.0f,0.0f]}
