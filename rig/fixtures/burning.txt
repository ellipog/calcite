# Fire, lava and water acting on what's in them (D434), in the flat world's chunk (0, 0) by the
# spawn, each mob penned in glass two high: a cow in soul fire on soul soil, which lights it and
# hurts it till it dies; a pig in lava, which lights it and hurts it more; a chicken on a lit
# campfire, which hurts it as it stands on it; and a sheep already alight, which panics to the
