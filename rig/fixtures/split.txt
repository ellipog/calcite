# A slime splitting as it's taken out dying (D430), in the flat world's chunk (0, 0) by its spawn:
# a slime of size 2 without AI, silent, made to stay and tagged, inside a layer of stone, which
# suffocates it till it dies. Taken out 20 ticks after, it splits into 2 to 4 of size 1, drawn
# from its random, each with what it was made of, `convertCommon`, inside the stone too, where
# they suffocate in turn and, of size 1, don't split. Built on easy, as peaceful summons no
# monsters.
difficulty easy
setworldspawn 0 -60 0
fill 2 -60 2 6 -60 6 minecraft:stone
summon minecraft:slime 4.5 -60 4.5 {Size:1,NoAI:1b,Silent:1b,PersistenceRequired:1b,Tags:["parent"]}
