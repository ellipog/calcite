# Arrows shot straight up as the world loads, each falling back some 80 ticks later, after the
# levels are seeded again, onto a block that does something as an arrow hits it, `onProjectileHit`
# (CAL-159), all in chunk (0, 0), on normal at midnight, none with an owner, so each may interact
# and break where it hits: one onto a block of amethyst, on dirt as grass under it would die on
# random ticks, which chimes, its pitch drawn from the level's random; burning ones onto a campfire
# and a candle that aren't lit, which they light; and ones onto a chorus flower on end stone, grown
# all it may, so it doesn't tick, and a decorated pot, which they break, the pot cracked first, so
# it drops its sherds. The bot stands at the spawn, the middle of the chunk.
