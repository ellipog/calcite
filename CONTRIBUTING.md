# Contributing

Issues, ideas and pull requests are welcome.

Before your first pull request can be merged, you agree to Calcite's [Contributor License Agreement](docs/CLA.md), once. A check on the pull request asks you to: comment the sentence it gives, and it records that you agreed. You keep the copyright in what you write.

Calcite is under the Business Source License, and Aaen Studios licenses it to hosting companies. The agreement lets your work go into those licenses, and into the MIT release every version becomes after two years.

Plugins aren't contributions to Calcite. They use the MIT plugin API, can have any license, and need no agreement.
