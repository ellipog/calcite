# Contributing

Issues and ideas are welcome now. Pull requests from outside open once the [Contributor License Agreement](docs/CLA.md) is in place. From then on, the CLA Assistant bot asks you to sign it on your first pull request, which takes one click with your GitHub account.

Calcite is under the Business Source License, and Aaen Studios sells licenses to hosting companies. The agreement lets your work go into those, and into the MIT release every version becomes after two years. You keep the copyright in what you write.

Plugins aren't contributions to Calcite. They use the MIT plugin API, can have any license, and need no agreement.
